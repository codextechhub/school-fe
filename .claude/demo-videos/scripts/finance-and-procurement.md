# Two explanatory videos: school fees, and buying for the school

Both videos are about 2 minutes 30 seconds, filmed on the local Lagoon View
Academy data in the same style as the parent, student and school videos: the
real app in a frame, a step counter, a large title and one plain line under it.
Each scene names whose screen it is, and the badge beside the school name
changes with the person's role ("Finance Admin", "Procurement Admin", "Parent app" and so on),
so a viewer always knows whose side they are on.

Where a person's screen is a phone (the parent), the stage switches to the
phone frame for that scene and back to the browser window afterwards.

---

## Video 1: School fees, from bill to ledger (about 2:30)

**The story.** It is the start of First Term at Lagoon View Academy. Ngozi
Ibekwe, the school's Finance Admin, sets when fees fall due, bills JSS1 A for the term,
Patricia Okafor pays the last of her son's fees from her phone, a father pays
cash at the school office, and Ngozi watches every naira land in the pupils'
statements and the school's books.

**The invoice reaches the guardian.** When a fee is raised for a pupil, the
invoice is sent to the pupil's guardian as well: Chidinma's bill reaches her
mother, Ngozi Eze, the moment the class is billed. Scene 6 shows it arriving,
and scene 7 opens on the parent app with the new bill already waiting.

*Not built yet.* Today raising a fee sends the guardian nothing. The invoice
email goes only to a billing email on the pupil's finance record, which billing
never fills, so it fails silently, and the parent app shows the bill only when
the parent happens to open Fees. This has to be built before scene 6 can be
filmed.

**Why these pupils.** Chidinma Eze is the pupil enrolled in the school-side
video, so the two videos join up. Tunde Adeleke owes exactly ₦1,687.50 and
Ebere Bello exactly ₦4,187.50 in the local data, so both payments clear a bill
on camera.

## Video 2: Buying for the school, ten microscopes for the lab (about 2:30)

**The story.** The Biology lab at Lagoon View has four working microscopes for
a class of forty. Mr Oluwaseun Bakare, Head of Sciences, asks for ten more. The
request goes to the Finance Admin and, because it is over ₦500,000, on to the
principal. The Procurement Admin gets three quotes and orders from the cheapest, the store
receives the delivery and sends one cracked microscope back, the supplier's
invoice is checked against the order and the delivery, and the Finance Admin pays it.

**Approvals off camera.** Purchase orders, invoices and payments each go
through the same two approvals as the request. Showing all of them would cost
about a minute, so the video shows the request's approvals in full (scenes 3
and 4) and, for the later documents, cuts straight to "Approved". The
approvals still happen in the data; they are just not filmed twice.

**The supplier's own view.** Suppliers quote through a link emailed to them,
on a page that lives in the console app. It can be filmed as a 10-second
insert inside scene 5 if wanted; the script above leaves it out to keep the
video at 2:30.
