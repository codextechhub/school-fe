# Two explanatory videos: school fees, and buying for the school

Both videos are filmed on the local Lagoon View Academy data in the same style
as the parent, student and school videos: the real app in a frame, a step
counter, a large title and one plain line under it. The fees video runs about
2 minutes 40 seconds and the procurement video about 3 minutes. Each scene names
whose screen it is, and the badge beside the school name changes with the
person's role ("Finance Admin", "Procurement Admin", "Parent app" and so on), so
a viewer always knows whose side they are on.

Where a person's screen is a phone (a parent), the stage switches to the phone
frame for that scene and back to the browser window afterwards.

---

## Video 1: School fees, from bill to ledger (about 2:40)

**The story.** It is the start of First Term at Lagoon View Academy. Ngozi
Ibekwe, the school's Finance Admin, sets when fees fall due and bills JSS1 A for
the term. Zainab's father finds the bill on his phone the moment it is raised.
Patricia Okafor pays the last of her son's fees from her phone, a father pays
cash at the school office, and Ngozi watches every naira land in the pupils'
statements and the school's books.

**The invoice reaches the guardian.** Every invoice, receipt, statement, note
and overdue reminder for a pupil goes to the pupil's primary guardian: by email
with the PDF, and, when they use the parent app, as a push that opens the
child's Fees. The parent app has no notification list of its own, so scene 7
shows the push as a banner on the drawn phone, in the push's own words, and then
the bill waiting in his app. Local email goes to the backend's log rather than
an inbox, so the email itself is named in the caption rather than shown.

**Why these people.** JSS2 A is the class whose pupils are on this year's roll
in the local data: most of the school's pupils have already been promoted into
next year's classes, so this year's other classes bill only one or two. Mr E.
Bello is the primary guardian of Zainab and Femi, billed in scene 5, and of
Ebere, who owes exactly ₦4,187.50 and is paid for in cash in scene 10; he has an
active parent app account. Tunde Adeleke owes exactly ₦1,687.50, and Patricia is
his primary guardian. Both payments clear a bill on camera.

## Video 2: Buying for the school, ten microscopes for the lab (about 3:00)

**The story.** The Biology lab at Lagoon View has four working microscopes for a
class of forty. Tolu Fashola, who teaches Biology, asks for ten more. Every
teacher can ask, and the request goes first to the person she reports to on the
school's organogram: Mr Oluwaseun Bakare, Head of Sciences. Finance approves it
next and, because it is over ₦500,000, the principal last. The Procurement
Admin gets three quotes and orders from the cheapest, the store receives the
delivery and sends one cracked microscope back, the supplier's invoice is
checked against the order and the delivery, and the Finance Admin pays it.

**Approvals off camera.** Purchase orders, invoices and payments each go
through approvals as the request does. The video shows the request's three
approvals in full (scenes 3 to 5) and, for the later documents, cuts straight
to "Approved". The approvals still happen in the data, through the same
approvals API the screens use; they are just not filmed again.

**Why the principal closes it.** The Approval Trail names each approver from
the school's staff list, which the Finance Admin role may not read, so on
Ngozi's screen the trail shows numbers where the names belong. The principal
reads the staff list, so the last scene is his.

**Quotes off camera.** Suppliers quote through a link emailed to them, on a page
that lives in the console app. The three quotes are entered between scene 6's
"Issue" and its comparison, so the comparison is real data.
