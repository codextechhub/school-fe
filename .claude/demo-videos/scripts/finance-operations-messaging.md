# Three more videos: the rest of finance, running the school, and messaging

Same stage, same school and same cast as the fees and procurement films:
Lagoon View Academy, the real app in a frame, a step counter, a large title,
one plain line under it, and Tessa's voiceover explaining a little more than
the caption. Each scene names whose screen it is in the badge beside the
school name. A phone slides in beside the window whenever a parent or a pupil
takes part.

These run longer than the first two because each covers a whole area.

| Video | Length | Scenes |
|---|---|---|
| 3. More than fees: the rest of the finance app | about 6:00 | 14 |
| 4. Running the school, from admission to results | about 7:00 | 15 |
| 5. Messaging: staff, parents and pupils | about 4:00 | 10 |

## Video 3: More than fees (about 6:00)

**The story.** The fees film showed money coming in. This one shows the rest
of Ngozi's month at Lagoon View: what the school planned to spend, what staff
claim back, the cash box in the office, paying salaries and the taxes on them,
checking the bank, helping families who cannot pay in full, and closing the
month with reports the board can read.

**Cast.** Ngozi Ibekwe (Finance Admin), Tolu Fashola (Teacher), Emeka Obi
(Principal, approver).

| # | Whose screen | Title | Line under it | What happens on screen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | (title) | More than fees | The rest of a bursar's month: spending, salaries, the bank and the books. | Opening card. |
| 2 | Finance Admin | The morning view | Cash in the bank, what families owe, and how many months the cash will last. | Finance dashboard: cash, receivables, collection rate, "Cash, spend & compliance" with the cash runway. |
| 3 | Finance Admin | Spending against the plan | The board approved a budget for the year. Every naira spent is measured against it. | Budgets: the 2026 operating plan, then the Science lab line at 22% used after the microscopes. |
| 4 | Teacher, then Principal, then Finance Admin | Staff claim it back | Tolu bought lab chemicals with her own money. She claims it, the principal approves, finance pays her. | Tolu's Expense claim with the receipt photo; the principal approves; Ngozi pays it from the bank. |
| 5 | Finance Admin | The cash box | Small payments come out of a float that is counted and topped up. | Petty cash: the office float, a voucher for a plumber, the count, and a top-up from the bank. |
| 6 | Finance Admin | Paying the staff | Salaries are worked out from each person's pay structure, with tax and pension taken off. | Payroll: salary structures, the October run calculated, one payslip opened, run approved and posted. |
| 7 | Finance Admin | The taxes on salaries | PAYE and pension are set aside from the run, ready to remit. | Statutory returns from the run, then Tax remittance: PAYE and pension obligations with a filing pack. |
| 8 | Finance Admin | Checking the bank | The bank statement is matched against the books, line by line. | Bank reconciliation: statement imported, Auto-match pairs the receipts and payments, a ₦1,075 bank charge added as an adjusting entry, reconciliation completed. |
| 9 | Finance Admin, then Principal | A scholarship | A pupil's fees are reduced, and anything above the limit needs a second approver. | Concessions: a 50% scholarship for a JSS2 pupil, sent to the principal, approved, and the bill reduced. |
| 10 | Finance Admin | Fixing a bill, and paying in parts | A wrong charge is corrected, and a family is allowed to pay in three instalments. | A credit note removes a library charge; a payment plan splits another bill into three dated parts. |
| 11 | Finance Admin | Gentle reminders | Bills past their due date are grouped by age, and families are reminded on a schedule. | Dunning: aging buckets, the reminder policy, the queue of families due a reminder. Run reminders. |
| 12 | Finance Admin | What the school owns | The bus, the generator, the lab equipment: each loses value each month, and the books record it. | Fixed assets register with net book value; run October's depreciation. |
| 13 | Finance Admin | Reports for the board | Every report comes from the same ledger, ready to print or export. | Trial balance, income statement against budget, balance sheet, cost-centre spend; export a PDF. |
| 14 | Finance Admin | Closing the month | Once September is closed, nobody can post into it, and every change is on record. | Periods: soft-close September; the audit trail of who did what. |

**Left out on purpose.** Inter-branch screens (they say the school has one
branch while the data has two; see "Found while surveying") and the payment
provider's settlement screens, which are about the provider, not the school.

## Video 5: Messaging (about 4:00)

**The story.** Who can talk to whom at Lagoon View, and why it stays safe:
teachers and families about one child, pupils with their teachers, staff
groups and announcements, and a principal who can look in where the rules
allow it.

**Cast.** Tolu Fashola (Teacher), Mr Bello (parent app), Femi Bello (student
portal), Chukwuemeka Eze (Branch Admin), Emeka Obi (Principal).

| # | Whose screen | Title | Line under it | What happens on screen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | (title) | Messaging at school | Teachers, families and pupils, each able to reach only the people they should. | Opening card. |
| 2 | Teacher | One inbox | Direct messages, family conversations, groups and announcements, sorted for her. | Tolu's inbox tabs. |
| 3 | Teacher | Writing to a parent | Tolu can write only to the families of children she teaches. | New message: the picker offers only her pupils' guardians; she writes to Mr Bello about Femi's practical. |
| 4 | Parent app (phone) | The parent answers | Mr Bello is told on his phone, opens it, and replies. Tolu sees it has been read. | Push, the thread in the parent app, the reply; Tolu's ticks turn to two. |
| 5 | Parent app (phone) | Who a parent can reach | The class teacher, Femi's subject teachers, and the school office. Nobody else. | New message in the parent app: the list of people he may write to. |
| 6 | Student portal (phone) | Pupils can ask too | Femi asks his teacher a question, and is told his parent and the school can read it. | Student portal: Femi writes to Tolu; the notice; his father sees it in the app, read only. |
| 7 | Branch Admin | Staff groups | A group for the JSS2 teachers. People added later see only what follows. | New group, members added, a message, "Read by 2 of 4". |
| 8 | Branch Admin | Announcements to staff | One message to every teacher. Replies come back privately to the sender. | Broadcast to all teaching staff with private replies; the recipient sees only a count. |
| 9 | Teacher | A private reply | Tolu answers the announcement. Only the sender sees it. | Tolu replies to the broadcast. |
| 10 | Principal | Keeping it safe | The principal can read groups, announcements and pupils' conversations, and every look is recorded. | Oversight: the group and Femi's thread, the "Every opening is recorded" banner, no box to type in. |

**Messages to every parent in a class** are not part of messaging: they are
Notices. The closing line says so; Notices could be its own short video.

## Getting the data ready

Every film needs the school's records to look like a real school's, and the
local Lagoon View data does not today:

- **Pupils:** most of this year's pupils sit in next year's draft classes, and
  42 names are duplicated, so a class list on screen shows doubles or is
  nearly empty.
- **Finance:** payroll, expense claims, petty cash, fixed assets, a bank
  statement, concessions, credit notes, payment plans and reminders are all
  empty.
- **Messaging:** the inbox holds test messages ("Probe 51815", "Double click
  test ...") that the app has no way to delete.
- **Teachers:** Tolu is not a class teacher, has no lesson today and no open
  score sheet.
